The Vision

A sanctuary, carved one prayer at a time.

Stone temples are not built in a year. They are an inheritance — quarried by devotion, raised by faith, and offered to generations yet to come. The Sri Sri Radha Madhava Stone Temple at ISKCON Salem is currently under construction, rising in eight blessed stages. Every stone placed here carries a name, a prayer, and a soul's offering to the Lord.
