Home · Construction Updates

From quarry to sanctum.

The temple is being raised in eight stages, each blessed by senior devotees and witnessed by a growing family of patrons. Here is where we stand today.

2023 · January

Bhumi Puja & Foundation

The first stones were sanctified, the land consecrated. Senior devotees performed the foundation ceremony before a full assembly of devotees.

Completed
2023 · Q3

Sub-structure & Plinth

Reinforced foundations completed; plinth raised in granite. The temple footprint takes shape on site.

Completed
2024 · Q1

Pillar Carving

108 hand-carved stone pillars commissioned from a heritage śilpi family — each pillar takes weeks of devotional carving.

Completed
2024 — 2025 · Ongoing

Mandapa Walls & Mandala Floor

Pillars are being raised and the mandapa walls assembled. Sacred geometry is being inlaid into the floor of the main hall.

Current phase
2026 · Planned

Garbha Griha & Gopuram

The inner sanctum is sealed and the gopuram begins its ascent — the temple's silhouette finally rises against the Salem sky.

Upcoming
2027 · Planned

Finishing, Painting & Deity Installation

Final stone polishing, traditional kalasham consecration, and the most awaited day — the installation of Sri Sri Radha Madhava.
